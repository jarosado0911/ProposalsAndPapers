The charge unit for SDSC Comet is the Service Unit (SU). One SU corresponds to the use of one compute
core for one hour (The minimum charge for a job longer than 10 seconds is 1 SU). Accordingly the
 requested SUs for the sub-projects in this proposal are summarized in Tab. \ref{tab:sus}, which 
have been determined by the following equation: $$SUs = \sum Runtime (Wall time)~[h]\quad \times\quad \#~Cores\quad \times\quad \#~Runs$$

The total storage requirement for the two sub-projects is \textbf{4.8 TiB}, in which the 
sub-project \ref{sec:spine-model} requires \textbf{2 TiB} and sub-project \ref{sec:whole-cell} requires \textbf{2.8 TiB} of storage.
